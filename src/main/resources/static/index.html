CogniFlow is an AI-powered Market Intelligence engine designed to capture, synthesize, and query financial trends.

Here is how the pipeline operates behind the scenes:

  • Ingestion Pool: The system automatically tracks a list of stock symbols (configured in the Live Ingestion Pool).
  • Alpha Vantage Integration: The background processor retrieves the latest stock quotes and price snapshot metrics.
  • Gemini 2.5 Flash: A generative AI process summarizes the trends into concise, qualitative vibes.
  • Weaviate Vector DB: Insights are converted into mathematical vector representations (embeddings) and persisted.
  • Hybrid Semantic Search: When you search, CogniFlow combines semantic vector matching and BM25 keywords to surface highly relevant matches instantly.
